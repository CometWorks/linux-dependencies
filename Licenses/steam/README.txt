The files in the parent directory are each governed by their own licence.

redistributing them as part of a Space Engineers Linux bundle.

Files in this directory:

This is the Steam companion archive (steam-dependencies.tar.gz), consumed
alongside se1-dependencies.tar.gz or se2-dependencies.tar.gz by any bundle
that integrates with Steam.
